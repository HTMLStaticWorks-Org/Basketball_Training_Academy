Chapter 01 // The Origin Story

Born From a Obsession With Pure Basketball Craft

COURTLY was founded in 2018 by Head Coach Marcus Hayes alongside a group of former collegiate standouts and biomechanists who recognized an alarming flaw in modern youth athletics: players were playing hundreds of chaotic tournament games every year without ever learning the foundational biomechanics of footwork, shot consistency, and spatial decision-making.

What started inside a rented single-court gymnasium in downtown New York has grown into an internationally respected basketball institution encompassing over 45,000 square feet of FIBA hardwood courts, automated shooting labs, and dedicated sports science clinics.

Chapter 03 // The Scientific Method

Training Philosophy &
Development Approach

We reject high-volume, unfocused repetitions that reinforce improper mechanics. Instead, Courtly's approach dissects every move into micro-phases: the balance base, explosive first-step angle, release arc, and landing stability.

01

Game-Speed Simulation

No static drills. Every ball-screen read, pull-up jumper, and defensive rotation is conducted against real resistance and live timers.

02

Deceleration & Joint Longevity

80% of basketball injuries occur during landing and deceleration. We teach athletes how to stop on a dime with zero knee strain.

03

Mental Toughness & Film IQ

Weekly film breakdowns teach players to see the game one second ahead of the defense, anticipating passes and traps before they happen.
