// 04 — The Methodology

The 4-Step Development Pipeline

Every trainee undergoes our proprietary development protocol to guarantee measurable, repeatable progress.

01

Kinematic Assessment

We test initial baseline vertical jump, lane agility, shooting arc, and sprint speed to generate an athlete biomechanical profile.

Week 1 // Diagnostic
02

Micro-Skill Blueprint

Tailored drills target individual weaknesses—whether eliminating wasted dip motions on catch-and-shoots or lateral slide footwork.

Weeks 2-6 // Isolation
03

Contested Game Speed

Introducing live defenders, timed shot clocks, physical contact finishing, and high-stakes scrimmage situations.

Weeks 7-10 // Pressure
04

Tournament Showcase

Placement in official Courtly Academy leagues with live video scouting reels distributed to collegiate networks.

Week 11+ // Competition
