Zone 01 // The Hardwood

FIBA Regulation Maple Courts

Our 4 primary hardwood courts are surfaced with Robbins All-Star Plus Grade-A Northern hard maple. Built with multi-layer resilient bio-cushion underlayment, our courts reduce lower back strain, shin splints, and joint fatigue by up to 58%.

Glare-Free Musco LED Sports Illumination Broadcast-standard 1500-lux flicker-free lighting optimizing tracking for high-speed automated cameras.
Dedicated HEPA-Filtered Climate Control Maintained at an optimal 68°F (20°C) with 45% ambient humidity to eliminate condensation on the hardwood floor.
Spalding Arena Pro 8400 Breakaway Rims Pro-caliber spring-action hydraulic goals calibrated to exact NCAA regulation tension.
Zone 02 // Power & Explosiveness

Kinetic Strength & Conditioning Lab

Basketball requires rotational torque, rapid change-of-direction, and deceleration control. Our 12,000 sq ft conditioning room is engineered without generic bodybuilding machines—favoring free weights, pneumatic resistance, and high-traction sprint turf.

Eleiko IWF Racks

8 dedicated Olympic lifting platforms with bumper plates for explosive clean and snatch training.

Keiser Pneumatic

Compressed-air resistance machines allowing athletes to train power at true game-speed velocities.

VertiMax Platforms

Multi-point cord resistance systems for lateral quickness and vertical leap expansion.

40-Yard Sprint Turf

High-density shock-absorbing turf lane for sled pushes, speed ladder drills, and combine testing.

Equipment & Practice Labs
